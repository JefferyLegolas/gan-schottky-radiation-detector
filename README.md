# GaN Schottky Radiation Detectors

An evidence-grounded research website for Tianli Shi's final-year project on quasi-vertical GaN Schottky barrier diodes for future space-radiation detector applications.

## Live website

https://gan-schottky-radiation-detector.jeffreylegolas.chatgpt.site

## Research scope

The project compares GaN devices with and without SiO₂ passivation through forward I–V, C–V and reverse-current transport measurements from 300–500 K.

The study establishes electrical device behaviour and a passivation trade-off. It does **not** report particle irradiation, detector sensitivity, charge-collection efficiency or energy resolution.

## Core findings

- SiO₂ passivation raises the reported turn-on voltage from 0.96 V to 1.46 V at 100 A/cm².
- At 500 K and 1.0 V, reported on-resistance increases from 2.02 to 4.22 mΩ·cm².
- The extracted Schottky barrier height is higher after passivation.
- Donor concentration and E_C − E_F remain nearly unchanged.
- Variable-range hopping is identified as the dominant reverse-current mechanism.
